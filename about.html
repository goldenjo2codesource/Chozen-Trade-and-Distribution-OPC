About Us

A company built for reliability and results

CHOZEN Trade and Distribution OPC is a dynamic and service-driven trade and logistics company based in the Philippines. We specialize in connecting global suppliers with local markets by providing reliable sourcing, efficient logistics, and tailored supply chain solutions.

Founded on the principles of transparency, reliability, and customer satisfaction, CHOZEN was established to address the growing demand for a more dependable and service-oriented approach to trade and distribution.

We believe that access to quality materials, products, and logistics services should never be a barrier to business success. Our operations span multiple industries — including construction, manufacturing, retail, and general commodities — and we pride ourselves on delivering not just products, but complete solutions that add value to our clients' businesses.

🎯

Mission

To efficiently connect global suppliers with local markets by delivering quality products and reliable distribution services, built on trust, integrity, and long-term partnerships.

🌟

Vision

Empowering supply chains by connecting global sources with local demand through smart, reliable distribution.

Our Story

Established in 2025

CHOZEN Trade and Distribution OPC was founded by Mr. Julieto "JOJO" Cañizares as Chief Executive Officer & General Manager.

The company was established in partnership to provide solutions that support the needs of all industries — manufacturing, construction, hotel, and more. The company's focus is on developing and delivering effective solutions to meet each client's operational demands.

From day one, CHOZEN has operated with one clear goal: make quality products and reliable logistics accessible to every business, regardless of size.
