About Us

Where People and Purpose Align

Chozen Resources Inc. provides dependable manpower and subcontracting services for manufacturing, construction, and operations teams.

Company Background

Built on dedication and strong values

The challenging economic conditions we face today have been the driving force behind the establishment of CHOZEN RESOURCES INC., a contracting and subcontracting services company founded by two professionals whose diverse careers served as the catalyst for its creation. Built through prayer, dedication, and hard work, the company is a service-oriented enterprise committed to providing quality general services that address the manpower needs of various organizations. Its primary goal is to help companies minimize the high costs associated with manpower recruitment and retention, thereby supporting investors in achieving operational efficiency and sustainability.

CHOZEN is bonded by a common philosophy, that is "To be investor's partner in productivity".

CHOZEN's growth can be attributed by its people. They are our greatest asset. Combined with quality excellence, cost reduction, delivery on time, and high morale — we exist to give you excellent service.

Company Objectives

What we work toward every day

✓
Quality Excellence

Product quality is assured through long-term Chozen workers who maintain performance, are familiar with quality checkpoints, perform visual checks during processing, and implement ISO quality standards.

✓
Cost is Low

HR saves time and money on recruitment, placement, and training. Fewer process rejects from experienced workers reduces material waste, training costs, and process costs — achieving better profit margins.

✓
Delivery Just in Time

Experienced and process-expert workers ensure complete and timely delivery to customers. High productivity results in on-time delivery every time.

✓
Morale is High

Orientation in PDCA, Horenso, Work Values, 5S, Safety, Ishikawa, and Teamwork boosts morale. Multi-skilled development enhances confidence, while reasonable compensation fosters pride and satisfaction.
